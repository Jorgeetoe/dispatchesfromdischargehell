## What Is Telos?

Telos (τέλος) — Greek for end, purpose, or ultimate aim. In ethics and philosophy, it refers to the ultimate goal or the highest human good that a thing or person is meant to achieve.

This site has one.

## The Purpose

**To document how inpatient rehabilitation discharge actually works in America — not how it's supposed to work, but how it *does* work.**

Specifically:
- The mechanics of insurance decision-making in discharge
- The timeline economics of bed occupancy and placement
- The gap between clinical needs and payer authorization
- Why families feel powerless (and often are)
- Where leverage points exist when they do
- What patterns repeat across cases, facilities, and payers

## The Audience

This is written for:

- **Families** who've just learned their loved one is being discharged somewhere they're unprepared for, at a timeline they don't understand
- **Clinicians** who know the system is broken but are too busy living inside it to articulate why
- **Case managers** who've spent years solving the same problems and want to see them documented somewhere public

This site is **not** written for insurance executives, hospital administrators solving for bed turnover, or anyone who benefits from the current system remaining opaque.

## The Voice

I write like someone who's been in the room. Not like a reformer. Not like someone making a principled argument. Like someone who's sat in 10,000 family conferences, read 50,000 pages of insurance denial language, and watched the same pattern play out in variations across two decades.

Dark humor, operational realism, and specific examples replace inspiration and hope. Hope is for people who haven't yet learned how the system works. This site is for people who need to survive inside it.

## What This Is Not

This is not:
- A policy paper proposing solutions
- A medical advice column
- A legal resource (though legal questions will be discussed)
- A rating system for facilities or insurers
- A replacement for professional consultation

It is documentation. Mechanics. The rules beneath the surface, written down.

---

Telos is also the reason every post that appears here will be true to the actual mechanics of the system, even when the actual mechanics are absurd. Especially then.
