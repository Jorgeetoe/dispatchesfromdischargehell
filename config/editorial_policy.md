# Editorial Policy — Dispatches from Discharge Hell
# Updated: February 28, 2026

## Hard Rules (non-negotiable)

### HIPAA
- Zero patient identifiers in any published content
- All scenarios are composites or fully de-identified
- If you see what looks like PHI, STOP. Do not generate HTML. Flag to Jorge.

### Legal
- No direct naming of specific insurance companies in satirical content (use patterns, not names)
- No direct naming of specific facilities in critical content
- Fictional stand-ins acceptable (e.g., "Last Resort Rehab Hospital")
- Systemic critique with constructive elements — not personal attacks

### Quality Gate
- Jorge's final approval is required on all published content
- No automated publishing without human review
- If Jorge pastes it and says publish, it's approved

## Soft Rules (editorial standards)

### Content Strategy (locked Feb 25, 2026)
- Blog only right now. No LinkedIn content until 100 posts published.
- Volume goal: 350-500 posts by year-end 2026.
- Primary audience: Families first, clinicians right behind.
- Blog feeds the book project. Every post is raw material.
- Success metric: Jorge reads, barely edits, posts.

### SEO Standards
- Every post needs: title tag (<60 chars), meta description (150-160 chars), target keyword
- Internal linking: 2-3 related posts per article where natural
- Schema markup: Article type with author, date, description
- Open Graph tags for social sharing

### Formatting Standards
- Static HTML/CSS only — no frameworks, no JavaScript dependencies
- Match existing site design exactly
- Responsive (works on mobile)
- Clean, readable typography — content-first design
- Categories displayed with post: Dispatches, Field Notes, The Machine, Case Files, Persona

### What NOT to Do
- Don't rewrite the draft. It arrives finished.
- Don't add content that wasn't in the draft.
- Don't change voice, tone, or word choices.
- Don't add images unless Jorge provides them.
- Don't create new CSS classes without reason.
- Don't add analytics, tracking scripts, or third-party code without explicit instruction.
