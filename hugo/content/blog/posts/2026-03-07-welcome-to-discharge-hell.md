I'm Jorge Arenivar. Registered nurse. 20+ years in catastrophic neurorehabilitation—TBI, SCI, disorders of consciousness. Last decade, case management at one of the top rehab hospitals in the country.

This blog tells you what actually happens when the hospital needs your bed.

## What This Is

Not the version on the website. Not the marketing brochure. Not the lobby wall success stories. Not "we take the hardest cases"—which is true, but which leaves out everything that happens after they arrive.

This is what happens when a family walks through the door expecting four to six months of rehab and you have three weeks. What happens when insurance decides your loved one has "plateaued" while you're watching them squeeze your hand for the first time. What happens when the skilled nursing facility says yes on the phone and calls back two hours later to say no.

## The Patterns

Over the next weeks, I'm walking through the recurring patterns I've watched play out across hundreds of catastrophic discharges. These aren't isolated incidents. They're not bad luck. They're structural—built into the way the system operates, repeated so consistently that I can predict the trajectory from the preadmission assessment.

Welcome to Discharge Hell. This is where the real work starts.
