## New to the Site?

This site contains five categories of content, each with a different purpose:

### Dispatches
Dark humor, satire, and stories about system absurdity. The kind of thing you laugh at because the alternative is crying.

### Field Notes
Practical guidance for families navigating the healthcare system and clinicians in the trenches. Actionable, specific, based on decades of pattern recognition.

### The Machine
Institutional mechanics: how insurance denials work, what administrators actually incentivize, why the system behaves the way it does. System analysis and critique.

### Case Files
De-identified pattern stories from 20+ years in catastrophic neurorehabilitation. Not one patient's story, but patterns that repeat across hundreds of cases.

### Persona
Off-duty reflections. The human side. What it costs to work in this field.
