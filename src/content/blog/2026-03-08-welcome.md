## What This Is

This blog is for the truth about discharge planning that doesn't fit into hospital marketing. The gaps between what families expect and what insurance funds. The census pressures tied to capital investment. The patients and families facing bad news, impossible choices, and system gaslighting.

## No Corporate Speak

When insurance denies a placement, I say insurance denied it. Not "authorization was not obtained" or "certain challenges presented themselves." When the hospital admits beyond capacity to justify capital investment, that's what happened.

Talk like a colleague, not a keynote speaker. Real examples over abstractions. "I don't know" beats bullshitting.

## The Scope

This covers catastrophic discharge planning—TBI, SCI, DOC cases. The mechanics of post-acute placement. Insurance nightmares (Medicare, Medicaid, commercial payers, TPAs, COBRA families). The reputation gap between marketing and reality. Institutional economics dressed as mission focus.
